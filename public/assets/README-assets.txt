Art provenance
- assets/units/*.png, ui/, deco/: AI-generated (image generation tool) for this project; cut from sheets in assets-src/<kind>/v2 (scripts/prep_ui_v2.py).
- assets/3d/*.png: buildings and the sword troop rendered from AI-generated 3D models (assets-src/models, tools/render3d.ts).
- assets/ui/hall, gacha/, maps/: AI-generated for this project (scripts/prep_hall.py, prep_gacha*.py, prep_maps.py).
- Earlier prototypes used CC0 packs (Kenney "Isometric Buildings", Jetrel "Japanese buildings") and a first, low-resolution generated set; neither is shipped any more.
